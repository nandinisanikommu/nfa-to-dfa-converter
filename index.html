NFA to DFA Converter

Convert a nondeterministic finite automaton into an equivalent DFA with a clear, classroom-style solution: transition tables, subset construction calculations, and state diagrams.

Define your NFA

Enter states and transitions. One transition per line.

Use comma-separated symbols, e.g. 0,1. Epsilon transitions are supported with ε.
Format: source,symbol,destination. Add separate lines for multiple transitions. Use ε for epsilon.
Tip: DFA states are sets of NFA states. A DFA state is accepting if its set contains at least one NFA final state.
